01

Shopping on JulaDirect

Finding products, comparing offers and talking to sellers.

How do I find a product?

Open Explore to browse the marketplace. Search by product name, brand or keyword, then narrow results using the available category, price, condition, location and delivery filters. Open a listing to read its full details and view its photos.

Can I compare products before buying?

Yes. Add products to Compare using the compare control on eligible product cards or product pages. Open Compare to review the selected listings side by side. You can also save items to your Wishlist.

How do I contact a seller?

Open the product page and select Message seller. Your conversations are available in Messages. Ask about availability, product condition, specifications, pickup arrangements or anything that is unclear before you order.

What does a verified seller badge mean?

The badge indicates that the seller's identity and business details have been checked by the JulaDirect team. It is a useful trust signal, but it is not a guarantee of a product's condition or a substitute for reading the listing and asking questions.

What if a product is unavailable or the details are unclear?

Do not assume an item is available just because you viewed it earlier. Message the seller to confirm stock and the exact specification before travelling for collection or placing an order. You can also browse similar listings in Explore.

How do I change items in my cart?

Open your cart to review quantities and remove items before checkout. Check the updated subtotal and delivery amount before continuing. Prices and stock are rechecked when you place an order.

02

Orders, delivery & pickup

What happens after you place an order and how to receive it safely.

Where can I see my order status?

Sign in and open My Orders. This is where you can review order details, available actions, delivery progress and the handoff-code option when it is ready. The seller is notified after an order is placed and confirms it before fulfilment proceeds.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery charges depend on the order and fulfilment arrangement. Review the delivery line in the checkout summary before placing the order. If the amount or delivery arrangement is unclear, ask the seller before confirming.

Which areas can receive delivery?

Delivery availability depends on the listing, seller and destination. Enter your delivery location during checkout and review the available fulfilment options. A seller may offer delivery for one listing and not another.

Can I collect an order instead of receiving delivery?

Where offered, choose Store pickup during checkout. The seller's pickup location is confirmed after they accept the order. Do not travel to a collection point until the seller has confirmed the location and collection arrangement.

When should I give the courier my delivery code?

Only after the order has physically reached you. Check that you have received the correct item, then share the one-time handoff code with the assigned courier to confirm delivery. Never send the code in advance, over chat or to someone who has not brought your order.

My delivery is late. What should I do?

Check the latest status in My Orders. Use Messages to contact the seller about the order. If the status does not explain the delay or you cannot resolve it with the seller, contact Customer Care with your order number.

03

Payments, coupons & refunds

Understand the options shown at checkout and what to do if something goes wrong.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

Cash on Delivery is not currently accepted by the checkout backend. Orders can be placed only with an online payment method that is enabled at checkout through ModemPay. Do not pay a seller or courier separately outside JulaDirect.

Can I pay with Wave, QMoney or Afrimoney?

Wave, QMoney and Afrimoney appear as selectable options only when the ModemPay configuration enables them for JulaDirect. If a method is disabled or no method is shown, online checkout is temporarily unavailable. Never send money to a personal number to release an order.

What makes up the amount I pay?

The checkout summary separates the merchandise subtotal, delivery charge, any applicable discount and the final total. Review that breakdown before you place the order. If the figures do not look right, return to your cart and check the items and quantities.

How do I use a coupon?

At checkout, open the coupon section, enter the code and select Apply. Check the message returned and make sure the discount appears in the order summary. A code may not apply if it has expired or its conditions are not met.

How do I request a refund?

Open the relevant order in My Orders. If a refund action is available for that order, submit the amount and a clear reason. Refund actions depend on the order and payment status; submitting a request does not mean it has already been approved. Keep photos or other evidence if the item is damaged or differs from its listing.

Can I cancel an order?

Open the order in My Orders and check whether the Cancel action is available. Cancellation depends on the order's current status. If the option is no longer available, contact the seller through Messages and ask Customer Care for help.

04

Selling on JulaDirect

Manage your listings, customer orders, COD reserve and seller wallet.

How do I start selling?

Open Seller Centre and sign in. Follow the seller setup and access steps shown there. Once your seller workspace is available, you can manage products, orders, customers, store details and earnings.

What information should I include in a product listing?

Use a precise product title, correct category, honest condition, accurate price and available stock. Add the product's location, a useful description and clear photos. Include important specifications, defects, included accessories and warranty details where relevant. Keep stock updated so buyers are not ordering unavailable items.

How many product photos can I upload?

The product form supports up to eight photos, with a maximum file size of 5 MB per image. Use clear, genuine photos of the actual item. Avoid misleading edits and make sure the first photo shows the product clearly.

Why is my product not visible yet?

New listings are reviewed before they go live in the marketplace. Check the product status in Seller Centre and make sure the title, category, price, stock, description and photos are complete. If a listing remains pending or needs attention, follow the status shown in your workspace.

How do I show that I can deliver a product?

When creating or editing a product, enable Delivery available only if you can arrange delivery for that listing. Delivery fees are confirmed at checkout. Keep the product's pickup location and delivery details accurate.

Where do I manage incoming orders?

Open the Orders area in Seller Centre. Review the customer, items, quantities, fulfilment choice and order status before taking action. Keep the status up to date and communicate with the buyer through Messages if you need to clarify anything.

How is the marketplace commission calculated?

The seller earnings screen shows a standard estimated marketplace commission of 5% on merchandise sales; delivery charges are excluded from that calculation. The amount shown against each transaction is the reference for that order, including any waiver or adjustment displayed there.

When can I withdraw my seller earnings?

Seller earnings remain pending until delivery is confirmed by both the courier and buyer, followed by the 24-hour dispute window. Once funds appear as available, you can request a withdrawal to a payout destination that has been approved by JulaDirect. Check the Seller Wallet for your current balances and transaction status.

Why can't customers choose Cash on Delivery?

COD must be enabled in your seller settings and your available reserve must be sufficient to cover the commission for the order. The seller wallet shows available and committed reserve. If the reserve is too low, buyers will not be offered COD for that order.

05

Couriers & marketplace workers

Applications, courier jobs and confirming a completed handover.

How do I apply to work with JulaDirect?

Visit Work with JulaDirect and submit an application for an available role, such as courier or marketplace operations. Follow the instructions shown during application and wait for the next steps or access decision.

What can a courier do in the workspace?

The courier workspace includes areas for jobs, the current run, earnings, delivery history and profile. Use the active job and order details shown in your workspace to complete each assigned delivery.

When should a courier ask for the buyer's handoff code?

Ask for the code only when you have reached the buyer and handed over the correct order. The buyer should inspect the delivery before sharing the code. Never ask for a code in advance or mark an order delivered before the handover is complete.

What if I cannot reach the buyer or find the address?

Use the contact and delivery instructions attached to the active order. Try the buyer using the available order contact details and follow the instructions in your workspace. Do not invent a delivery confirmation or ask another person to provide the buyer's code. Escalate an unresolved delivery issue through the work support channel provided to you.

Account, privacy & safety

Sign-in problems and simple ways to protect your account and orders.

I forgot my password. How do I get back in?

Open the Login page and choose the password recovery option. Follow the instructions sent to the email address associated with your account. If you no longer have access to that address, contact Customer Care.

Where can I update my account details?

Open My Account, then go to Profile & preferences in Settings to review the details available to edit. Keep your phone number and delivery information current so sellers and couriers can reach you about an order.

How do I keep my account and order safe?

Keep your password and sign-in codes private. Never share your order handoff code before the item is physically delivered. Only use payment methods shown as available in JulaDirect checkout, and be cautious of anyone asking you to pay an unrelated personal number to release an order.

Where can I read about my personal information?

Read the Privacy Policy for information about the personal details used to support accounts, orders, delivery and marketplace services. Contact Customer Care if you have a privacy-related question about your account.
